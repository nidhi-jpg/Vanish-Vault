Access & Verification Policy

This page explains who can access which features and what documents are required during registration.

Roles

  • Citizen: Public reporting and search
  • Volunteer: Supports NGOs; needs verification for found reports
  • NGO Worker: Organization-backed; can report found persons after verification
  • Police Officer: Case verification and management after verification
  • Admin: Full system access

What you can do

  • Everyone: View Home, Search, Missing/Found details.
  • Citizens: Report Missing person and submit Sightings of missing persons.
  • Authenticated: Report Missing Person
  • Verified NGO/Volunteer: Report Found Person
  • Verified Police/Admin: Dashboard and verification tools

Who verifies what

  • Admin: Reviews and verifies user documents for Police, NGO, and Volunteer accounts.
  • Police: Verify and approve cases in the Dashboard.

Verification requirements

  • Citizen: No documents. Auto-verified.
  • Volunteer: Organization required. Admin verification required.
  • NGO Worker: Organization and ID document required. Admin verification required.
  • Police Officer: Organization and official ID required. Admin verification required.

Notes

  • Until verification, non-citizen roles have limited access.
  • Admins review documents and approve verification.
