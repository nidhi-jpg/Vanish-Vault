How to Use VanishVault

A comprehensive visual guide to navigate and use all features of our platform

Back to Home
1

Search for Missing Persons

Browse our database to find missing persons or unidentified found individuals using advanced search filters.

  • Click "Search Database" in navigation
  • Filter by name, age, location, or date
  • View detailed case information and photos
Start Searching
2

Report a Missing Person

If someone you know is missing, report it immediately to get help from the community and authorities.

  • Click "Report Missing" button
  • Fill out the form with personal details
  • Upload clear photos and last seen information
Report Now
3

Report a Sighting

If you've seen someone who might be missing, your report could help reunite families.

  • Search for the missing person first
  • Click "Report Sighting" on their profile
  • You can choose to remain anonymous
Search & Report
4

Report a Found Person

If you've found someone who appears lost or unidentified, help them get home.

  • Available for verified NGO workers and volunteers
  • Click "Report Found" in navigation
  • System will check for potential matches automatically
Report Found
5

Use Face Search

Upload a photo to find potential matches using advanced facial recognition technology.

  • Navigate to "Face Search" in the menu
  • Upload a clear front-facing photo
  • Review and compare potential matches
Try Face Search
6

Access Dashboard

For verified police officers and admins - manage cases and verify reports efficiently.

  • Available for verified Police and Admin users
  • View statistics and case overview
  • Verify and approve missing person reports
Go to Dashboard

Quick Tips

Need an account? Register to report missing persons and access more features.

Privacy protected: All reports are handled securely and you can report sightings anonymously.

24/7 Available: Report missing persons or sightings at any time, day or night.

Need help? Check our Access Policy for role-based features.
